# Unity & Hope Owner Portal Guide

## Sign in

1. Open `https://admin.uhhomehealth.com`.
2. Enter your own approved administrator email and password. Passwords and sessions are never shared between administrators.
3. If the password is unknown, choose **Forgot password?**. If the email is authorized, a one-time link is sent only to that address and expires after 30 minutes. The portal always shows the same generic confirmation so it never reveals the allowlist.
4. Use **Sign Out** when finished, especially on a shared device.

There is no public registration page. Six unsuccessful sign-in attempts trigger a temporary 15-minute limit.

## Update the website

- **Website Content** updates core phone/email/address details, homepage messaging, About mission copy and homepage section visibility.
- **Services** adds, edits, reorders, hides or deletes services. Keep every service factual and clearly non-medical.
- **Service Areas** changes the area list. Do not publish a city/county until service availability is confirmed.
- **Team** publishes only approved names, roles, biographies and photos. Hidden profiles do not appear publicly.
- **Blog & Resources** creates drafts, edits accessible article sections and publishes approved educational articles. Do not add diagnosis, treatment advice or unsupported claims.
- **SEO** edits unique page titles and descriptions. Canonical paths and the production domain are protected.

Use JPEG, PNG or WebP images up to 4 MB. Add descriptive alt text that explains the image without claiming a pictured model is a real client or employee.

## Reviews

New reviews remain private and appear as **Pending**. Choose **Approve** to publish, **Decline** to reject, **Hide** to remove an approved review from the website, or **Publish** to restore a hidden approved review. Delete is permanent. The email approval/decline buttons remain available as a second secure moderation method.

Never edit or create a review on someone else's behalf. Pending, declined and hidden reviews never appear publicly.

## Inquiries and careers

**Submissions** contains Contact and Request Care records. **Applications** is the dedicated careers inbox. Open an item to review every submitted field, change its status, save private owner notes, send a secure follow-up email, view activity, archive/restore it, or download a validated résumé. Search and filters work independently in each section. Delete only when retention is no longer required; deletion permanently removes the record and attachment.

Use **New**, **Reviewing**, **Contacted** and **Closed** consistently. Sending a follow-up email automatically marks the record **Contacted** and records the sender, recipient, subject and time in its private activity history. The message body remains private and is not copied into the activity log.

The website continues to send the owner notification and visitor confirmation email. **Reply by Email** responds to the submitter because Reply-To is set to their address.

## Safe publishing habits

- Preview `https://uhhomehealth.com` after meaningful changes.
- Keep medical, licensing, coverage, credential and outcome claims limited to facts the business can verify.
- Do not paste Social Security numbers, financial information, medical records or passwords into website content.
- If a save reports a conflict, refresh the portal, review the latest content and apply the change again.
